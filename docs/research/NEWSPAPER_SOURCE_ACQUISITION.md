# Persian newspaper acquisition record

- **Audit date:** 2026-07-26
- **Queue ID:** `IR-P5`
- **Current result:** Newspaper coverage is broad but intentionally
  event-centered and incomplete. Existing files support situated claims from
  named publications; they do not form a neutral or continuous newspaper of
  record.

Every use requires title, issue/date, page or item, political position, and
comparison with scholarship or another position.

## Active event-window gaps

1. **October 1949:** *Shahed* and other complete issues around 12–24 October,
   especially 24 October for the National Front declaration.
2. **Thirty Tir:** a multi-title 17–24 July 1952 window, especially *Shahed*
   on 19 and 23 July and complete issues from both government-aligned and
   opposing positions.
3. **Nineteen August:** full 15–22 August 1953 windows from genuinely opposing
   positions, including complete 28 Mordad issues. Local complete issues reach
   28 Mordad for *Niruye Sevvom*, *Apadana*, and *Shahed*; *Ettela'at*,
   *Bakhtar-e Emruz*, and the complete *Kayhan* run do not.
4. **Aftermath:** *Shahed* for 29 and 31 Mordad and 1–3 and 9 Shahrivar,
   alongside full *Kayhan*, *Dad*, *Aram*, *Setareh-e Islam*, *Mard-e Asia*,
   and *Mellat-e Iran* issues.

## Online collections not yet ingested

- **Remaining *Ettela'at*:** Mashruteh exposes near-daily issue PDFs for 1329
  and 1330, a partial 1331 run through 31 Shahrivar, and a near-complete 1332
  run. The saved 1332 enumeration alone describes roughly four gigabytes, so
  event-first intake is preferable to indiscriminate copying. No 28–30 Mordad
  file is exposed.
- **Manchester Nashriyah:** the University of Manchester's [Iran in the 1950s
  collection](https://www.library.manchester.ac.uk/services/digitisation-services/projects/nashriyah-digital-iranian-history/iran-in-the-1950s/)
  exposes title/date-searchable *Shurish* and *Tihran-i Musavvar*. They offer
  another pro-Mossadegh radical position and a military/security-connected
  counter-position. The viewer did not expose a stable bulk file during the
  last audit; no issue is therefore cataloged as locally archived.
- **CRL *Kayhan*:** the [catalog
  record](https://catalog.crl.edu/Record/491a2481-7393-526e-91d8-fbe86138cc55)
  covers 22 December 1952–19 March 1953 and September–December 1953. Confirm
  open versus member-only access at object level.
- **Princeton digitized press:** the [pre-1979 Persian-newspaper
  project](https://dpul.princeton.edu/menam/feature/pre-1979-persian-newspapers-collection)
  includes 1951–53 youth and party titles. Its digitized volume 22 is a
  catalog trap for this campaign: metadata assigns later *Shahid* dates, but
  the inspected scans begin with no. 1, 5 Tir 1325, and the earlier weekly's
  staff. Do not use it as Baghai-era evidence.

## Reproduction and microfilm routes

- **Princeton `MICROFILM S00895`:** [catalog
  `9911646863506421`](https://catalog.princeton.edu/catalog/9911646863506421)
  holds five IDC *Shahid/Shahed* reels described as 1946–53, including the
  second-series opening and the current priority dates. Request by second
  series, exact Persian and Gregorian date, and call number.
- **Princeton `MICROFILM S01654`:** [catalog
  `9910776633506421`](https://catalog.princeton.edu/catalog/9910776633506421)
  records a longer Tehran University filming. Its note says four reels while
  five reel items are listed; verify the target on film rather than trusting
  the summary count.
- **University of Chicago popular press:** the [microfilm
  list](https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/e/collections/mideast/poppress/MidEast_PopularPress_1995list_2015version.pdf)
  identifies *Bakhtar-i Imruz* (`P30.1–7`), *Shahid* (`P216.1–5`), *Dad*
  (`P129.1–8`), *Atish* (`P11.1–2`), *Bisuy-i Ayandah* (`P127`),
  *Nabard-i Millat* (`P85.1`), and *Shallaq* (`P217`). Request exact dates,
  not whole runs.
- **Princeton volume 50:** catalog `9911665783506421` contains *Mardom*
  (1951–53) and *Hajji Baba* (1950–53) but is not currently digitized.
- **Reported *Shahed* facsimile:** a bibliographic survey records a limited
  four-volume 1385 / 2006 photocopy edition covering 21 Shahrivar 1328 through
  20 Mehr 1331. No authoritative holding or public scan has been located; it
  remains a lead, not evidence.

## Use and intake gate

- Preserve full issue context, masthead, date, page sequence, carrier, and
  rights metadata.
- Distinguish editorial position, attributed reporting, advertisements,
  reproduced documents, photographs, and later annotations.
- Do not infer neutrality from circulation or completeness.
- Do not use archive or opposition-site summaries as authority for the
  underlying issue.
- Do not reacquire large runs merely to increase file count; intake follows a
  defined event, actor, organization, or claim gap.
