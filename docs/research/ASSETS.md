# Historical asset and rights ledger

No asset may be used in the game without an entry here. “Public domain” records
must identify the claimed jurisdiction or rationale; age alone is not enough.

## Status key

- **Approved** — provenance and reuse terms are adequate for prototype use.
- **Hold** — downloaded for research, but not cleared for use.
- **Candidate** — identified online but not downloaded or verified.

## Candidate and hold notes

- The 1951 International News Photos portrait of Mohammad Mossadegh on Commons
  is public domain in the United States because of publication formalities, but
  the Commons record warns that it may remain protected in jurisdictions that
  do not apply the shorter term. Keep it on hold until release jurisdictions
  are reviewed:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dr_Mohammad_Mosaddeq.jpg>.
- A 1954 Tehran bazaar photograph is close to the campaign period and marked
  public domain in Iran, but its photographer and original provenance are weak.
  It remains a candidate rather than an approved asset:
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bazaar_tehran1954.jpg>.
