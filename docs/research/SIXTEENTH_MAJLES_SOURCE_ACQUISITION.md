# Sixteenth-Majles source acquisition search

- **Search date:** 2026-07-26
- **Scope:** the Parliament Library conference volume on the Thirteenth
  through Sixteenth Majles and UK National Archives file `FO 371/75466`
- **Outcome:** both sources are precisely identified. No lawful, inspectable
  online copy of the complete Parliament Library volume or `FO 371/75466` was
  found. A complete-looking author-posted version of one priority chapter was
  recovered and archived as [`SUP-056`](AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md#sup-056). Two
  official volumes covering all three periods of the Sixteenth-Majles Special
  Oil Commission were then recovered and archived as
  [`SUP-059`](AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md#sup-059). These do not make the conference
  volume available, but they substantially replace its oil chapters as access
  routes to the underlying primary record. Neither unavailable target is
  treated as reviewed in full.

## Parliament Library conference volume

### Exact identity

*Nigāhī bih sākhtār va kārkard-i Majlis-i Shūrā-yi Millī: dawrah-i
sīzdahum tā shānzdahum: majmūʿah-i maqālāt-i Sivvumīn Nishast-i
Takhaṣṣuṣī-i Tārīkh-i Majlis* (نگاهی به ساختار و کارکرد مجلس شورای ملی
دوره‌های سیزدهم تا شانزدهم: مجموعه مقالات سومین نشست تخصصی تاریخ مجلس),
compiled by Ali Tatari and Hasan Zandiyeh and edited by Ruhollah Tahmuresi
(Tehran: Library, Museum and Documentation Center of the Islamic Consultative
Assembly, 1392 SH / 2013–14), 618 pp., publication no. 391.

- ISBN: `978-600-220-172-0`
- Iranian national bibliography number: `3176388`
- Library of Congress control number: `2013358066`
- Library of Congress call number: `JQ1787 .N57 2012`
- OCLC: `883726138`
- Open Library edition: `OL31264752M`
- Google Books ID: `Ms6b0QEACAAJ`

The Library of Congress record says the physical book includes
bibliographical references and is in Persian. Its public metadata marks the
item `digitized: false` and exposes no digital resource. Open Library supplies
a catalog record but no Internet Archive identifier or lending object. Google
Books supplies bibliographic metadata, but its public within-volume endpoint
reports the item as non-searchable.

Exact-title, ISBN, Internet Archive, Iranian journal-index, and general web
searches found catalog records, an out-of-stock bookseller listing, and the
published table of contents, but no scan of the volume. The Internet Archive
advanced-search catalog returned no matching item.

### Opposition-archive and chapter-level follow-up

A second archive-first pass checked:

- all three pages of Iran Archive's `ن–و` general-book title run;
- its relevant `ط`, `ت`, and `ز` author indexes for Ali Tatari, Ruhollah
  Tahmuresi, and Hasan Zandiyeh, including spelling variants;
- its National Front and Iran Party collections;
- exact title, ISBN, alternate title, editor, and six priority chapter-title
  searches in the public previews of Iran Archive's Telegram channel and the
  linked Iran-studies library channel;
- Mashruteh's public MediaWiki API for the same book, editor, ISBN, and
  chapter queries; and
- Iran Archive's linked public libraries, including Ketab Farsi, Ariaye,
  IranGlobal, and Rooznameha, through exact-title and chapter-title searches.

No copy of the book appeared in those catalogs. Mashruteh returned no matching
page for the book or priority chapters; its useful Sixteenth-Majles
proceedings remain separate primary records, not a substitute for the
collection. Iran Archive did expose other works on the nationalization
sequence, but not this volume.

One significant partial recovery was found outside those repositories:
Younes Sadeghi, a named coauthor, posted a complete-looking version of “Majlis
shānzdahum-i Shūrā-yi Millī va millī shudan-i ṣanʿat-i naft” (مجلس شانزدهم
شورای ملی و ملی شدن صنعت نفت) on his Blogfa site on 17 Mehr 1392. The text
contains a bibliography and forty numbered notes and matches one chapter in
the published contents. It is archived as
[`SUP-056`](AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md#sup-056).

This is an author-posted web version, not a paginated scan. The posted first
author's name is abbreviated, the text contains typographical and
bibliographic defects, and identity with the final edited chapter has not
been verified. It may be used as a lead with claim-level corroboration, but
the print volume remains necessary for final text, pagination, and the other
twenty-five chapters.

### Why the volume remains important

The published contents identify several potentially useful chapters:

- Nemat Ahmadi-Nasab and Younes Sadeghi, “The Sixteenth National Consultative
  Assembly and the Nationalization of the Oil Industry”;
- Ruhollah Bahrami and Mohammad Goudarzi, a statistical comparison of Majles
  deputies in the Fourteenth through Seventeenth terms;
- Rahim Rouhbakhsh, a documentary study of religious forces in the elections
  of the Thirteenth through Sixteenth terms;
- Leila Kaveh-Chelcheh and Ezzat Tahmasebi-Far, Mostafa Mesbahzadeh's political
  activity in the Fifteenth and Sixteenth terms;
- Zeynab Naseri-Liravi, “The Oil Commission and the Sixteenth Majles”; and
- Noor al-Din Nemati, the Sixteenth Majles and land reform.

The table of contents does **not** establish that the book contains a complete
member-by-member faction register. Each chapter's author expertise, method,
citations, and claims must be assessed after acquisition.

### Best acquisition route

1. Request the physical book through interlibrary loan or Library of Congress
   reproduction using LCCN `2013358066` and call number
   `JQ1787 .N57 2012`.
2. Ask compiler Ali Tatari directly through the public contact address on his
   professional site, `tatari.info@gmail.com`, whether he can supply an
   author/editor copy or identify a current Parliament Library download or
   distributor. Give ISBN `9786002201720`, publication no. 391, and national
   bibliography no. `3176388`.
3. Ask the Parliament Library publisher for a lawful electronic copy or
   reproduction using the same identifiers.
4. Prefer the complete volume because its notes and bibliography are part of
   the evidence audit. If only chapter copying is possible, start with the
   deputy-statistics, religious-election, Mesbahzadeh, and land-reform chapters
   and obtain each chapter's notes in full. The oil chapters are now second
   priority because `SUP-059` preserves the official commission record.

## UK National Archives file `FO 371/75466`

### Exact evidence sought

Ahmad Ashraf's specialist article on Abd-al-Hosayn Hazhir identifies two
documents in this file:

1. Le Rougetel to Wright, 19 July 1949, concerning Hazhir's court position and
   links to Razmara and Princess Ashraf's circle; and
2. Lawford to the Foreign Office, 13 October 1949, reporting Hazhir's attempts
   to interest the British Embassy in a Sixteenth Majles favorable to
   ratification of the Supplemental Oil Agreement and his question about
   former deputies who had opposed it.

These are the minimum known targets. The whole file, including enclosures,
registry slips, marginal minutes, and adjacent correspondence, is preferable:
the surrounding papers may identify candidates or reveal whether Embassy
officials acted on, rejected, or merely recorded Hazhir's approach.

### Online search result

Exact-reference, correspondent/date, quotation, Internet Archive, and
commercial archival-platform searches found no scan or transcription of the
file. The UK National Archives Discovery site was protected by a web
application challenge during this review, and its API now requires prior
access approval, so the exact catalogue page and digitization flag could not
be independently inspected. This record therefore says **no online copy was
found**, not that the file is definitively undigitized.

The Qatar Digital Library contains useful but non-equivalent India Office
material: a 1943 Hazhir memorandum, Tehran intelligence summaries running
through 21 May 1949, and British Library file `IOR/L/PS/12/1225`, now archived
as [`SUP-054`](AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md#sup-054), whose carrier dates extend to
24 October 1949. The last file overlaps the target correspondence in date and
subject, but local inspection did not find the two known July and October
documents from `FO 371/75466`. These are different archival series; none
contains or replaces the candidate-influence evidence sought from
`FO 371/75466`.

### Best acquisition route

Use the UK National Archives record-copying service with reference
`FO 371/75466`. A page-check request should give the two correspondents and
dates above and ask staff to include all attachments, minutes, and registry
pages belonging to each item. If copying the whole piece is practical, that is
preferable to isolated pages. The copying service is not a research service,
so the named dates and correspondents are essential request metadata.

`FO 371/75466` is an official contemporary British diplomatic file. It is
authoritative for what British officials received, reported, and discussed,
but not automatically for the truth of every allegation or for a candidate's
actual allegiance. Candidate identifications must be compared with Persian
election records, credentials, contemporary press, and specialist
scholarship.
