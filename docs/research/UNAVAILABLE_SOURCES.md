# Unavailable sources

This is the active register of authoritative or potentially authoritative
material whose necessary text is not inspectable in the current workspace.
It contains only residual evidence gaps. Acquired files belong in
[`AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md`](AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md); author expertise and source
standing belong in [`BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md); detailed searches
belong in the domain acquisition records listed in
[`README.md`](README.md#acquisition-search-records).

- **Last reconciled:** 2026-07-26
- **Access rule:** a catalog, snippet, finding aid, or carrier page can
  establish identity and a request route but cannot support a historical
  claim from the unavailable text.
- **Implementation rule:** no entry below is a blanket blocker. It blocks only
  the claim granularity named in its **Residual gap** column. The accessible
  bundles and safe substitutes are audited in
  [`ONLINE_SUBSTITUTION_AUDIT_2026-07-26.md`](ONLINE_SUBSTITUTION_AUDIT_2026-07-26.md).

## Queue maintenance

When material is acquired:

1. preserve the unchanged file and sidecar in the ignored local archive;
2. add its edition, provenance, checksum, and review state to
   `AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md`;
3. update its expertise and project-use record in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`;
4. remove the resolved item from this active queue rather than retaining a
   second acquisition history; and
5. reconcile every claim audit that depended on the former gap.

OCR remains a finding aid. Verify quotations, names, dates, numbers, and page
locators against the source image or authoritative electronic text.
