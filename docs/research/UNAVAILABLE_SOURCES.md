# Unavailable sources

This is the acquisition queue for sources that appear authoritative and highly
relevant but whose necessary text is not accessible in the current workspace.
“Unavailable” does not mean that no copy exists online. It means that the
project did not locate a lawful, usable full text during the most recent review,
or that available previews do not expose the pages needed for citation.

Files already held by the project are listed in
[`AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md`](AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md); expertise and trust assessments
for both available and unavailable works are in
[`BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md).

- **Last access review:** 2026-07-26
- **Rule:** a listing, catalog record, snippet, or publisher description can
  establish metadata and research priority, but cannot support a historical
  claim in the game.

When a source is acquired, record the exact edition and file provenance in
`AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md`, update its access status in `BIBLIOGRAPHY.md`, move its
queue entry into the resolved table below, and add page or chapter locators
only after the text has been checked.
