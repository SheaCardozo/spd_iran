# Online substitution audit for inaccessible priority sources

- **Review date:** 2026-07-26
- **Question:** If the remaining priority acquisitions cannot realistically
  be obtained, which accessible evidence can answer the same research
  questions, and what precision must the project give up?
- **Outcome:** None of the listed acquisitions is a general implementation
  blocker. Most intended claims can be supported by a convergent online/local
  bundle. The unavailable originals remain blockers only for narrower claims:
  named Senate divisions and speeches, a complete Sixteenth-Majles patronage
  register, account-exact economic mechanics, exact casualty ledgers and
  payee chains, and meeting-level World Bank attribution.

“Substitute” below does not mean that a later summary becomes the original
record. It means that the project can state a narrower proposition from
independent evidence while identifying what the missing source alone could
still add.

## First Senate: the practical replacement

The first six-month powers vote is the best example of a bounded substitute.
The official enacted-law record dates Senate approval to 20 Mordad 1331 /
11 August 1952. The Seventeenth-Majles record preserves the lower-house
standing vote but gives no number. A contemporary report in *Le Monde*,
attributed to AFP, United Press, and AP, reports that the Senate approved by
26 votes to 4 after first adjourning and sending a ten-member delegation to
clarify Mossadegh's intentions. FRUS and the specialist books independently
place the vote inside the post-30 Tir shift in institutional power.

That is enough to represent a reluctant upper chamber yielding after
consultation. It is not enough to color individual senators as yes/no voters,
assign abstentions, or write a verbatim debate. The same rule applies to the
other target sessions: use the enacted measure, lower-chamber record,
contemporary reporting, diplomatic observation, and specialist reconstruction
for institutional outcomes; reserve the Senate's own language for the missing
proceeding.

## Sixteenth Majles: use dated layers, not a synthetic party chart

The accessible evidence already establishes:

- 136 authorized places, 131 eventual returns, and 98 credentials in the
  first large approval wave;
- the delayed Kashan and Tehran credential waves;
- the National Front's small historical cohort of about eight returned
  deputies;
- Abrahamian's broad opening social-political groupings;
- named late-term anti-Mossadegh organizers in Azimi;
- unanimity among those present for the nationalization report and the
  nine-article implementation law; and
- recorded attendance, speech, office, credential, and decision behavior in
  the complete official corpus.

`FO 371/75466` would clarify what British officials heard from Hazhir and how
they discussed the oil-agreement election. It would not, by itself, prove that
every named or implied candidate was controlled by Britain. The two known
documents are quoted in specialist bibliographic literature, but under the
project's rules that tertiary route remains a lead rather than a claim source.
The defensible substitute is therefore the joined member/credential/vote
ledger with dated confidence labels—not an inferred royalist majority list.

## Economic originals: an observation spine without false exactness

The online institutional record is unusually good at preserving transmitted
Iranian data even when the originating publication is absent. `SUP-057`
retains four report vintages of IMF tables, definitions, footnotes, and
revisions. `SUP-058` supplies contemporary UN tables, including an official
foreign-assets table explicitly sourced to “Iran (Bank Melli and Treasury).”
The IBRD, IMF exchange-control, ILO, budget, and later Bank Melli-sourced
records form independent checks.

This bundle supports a game system based on direction and pressure:
falling foreign-exchange availability, import compression, fiscal strain,
continued oil-worker payroll, exchange control, uneven price and employment
effects, and delayed development. It does not justify a perfectly measured
monthly GDP series, exact Treasury cash balance, or province-level welfare
index. When sources disagree, store the observation vintage and range rather
than silently harmonizing them.

The UN's openly available *Statistical Yearbook* and 1952 Middle East survey
provide additional online table checks. They are useful at the individual
table level but do not outrank the already archived IMF/UN bundle merely
because another PDF is available.

## Crisis evidence: what the substitute changes

For 30 Tir, official Seventeenth-Majles session 22 records that the parliamentary
investigation treated deaths after 30 Tir as part of the same casualty class
and includes partisan partial figures for named organizations. That is useful
evidence about the inquiry and contemporary claims, not a national total.
The acquired books and bazaar study support the structural conclusion that
National Front deputies, clerical-bazaar networks, guilds, Tudeh/labor
organizations, and provincial actors mobilized through distinct channels.

For 19 August, `SUP-060` materially improves the accessible clock. It uses
contemporaneous BBC and FBIS monitoring plus Persian sources to separate:

1. government control of Radio Tehran before and after the failed first coup;
2. the ban on demonstrations broadcast on 18 August;
3. seizure of the studios around the early afternoon of 19 August;
4. named speakers and the sequence of royalist broadcasts;
5. false announcements of Fatemi's death and Mossadegh's house falling before
   either had happened; and
6. the spread of radio capture to provincial stations.

This sharpens a communications and state-control mechanic. It does not settle
street-crowd origins, casualty totals, or payments.

The University of Manchester's Nashriyah collection remains the best online
route to fuller opposing press: radical pro-Mossadegh *Shurish* and the
military-intelligence-connected *Tihran-i Musavvar* sit alongside the already
ingested left and Third Force titles. Its viewer was access-checked but did
not expose a stable bulk file during this audit, so no issue is falsely listed
as archived.

## Scholarship and World Bank: preserve disagreement

No full lawful online copy of Majd's 1995 rejoinder, Bamberg's volume, Movahed
volume I, or the two Chaqueri priority volumes was found. A search result that
appeared to expose Kamran Dadkhah's 1988 economic chapter proved on download
to be a two-page contents sheet, not the chapter; it was not archived or
promoted.

For the World Bank, the accessible evidence should be presented as a dispute,
not fused into one narrative. The Bank's contemporary documents and FRUS
establish terms and sequence. Katouzian argues that rejection was a grave,
avoidable mistake; Gorjestani argues from cited Bank files that the proposal
would have restored AIOC control under international cover. Their 2024–26
exchange exposes both the evidentiary and counterfactual disagreement. Neither
article replaces the missing meeting memoranda, and neither side's
counterfactual becomes fact.
