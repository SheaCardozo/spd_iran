# Allahyar Saleh — adviser claim record

Documented role: Iran Party and National Front figure returned for
Kashan/Natanz; his credential passed on 18 May 1950. The adviser abstracts
electoral and organizational work rather than a complete personal portfolio.

January availability is an opening-opposition abstraction grounded in the Iran Party's
documented movement toward a pro-Mossadegh position in 1947–1949 and Saleh's
role in that party. It does not claim a formal January adviser appointment or
a National Front organization before one existed.

Sources: `MAJ-S2`, chapter 5, pp. 249–267, especially pp. 249–253 for the
party's movement toward Mossadegh and pp. 250–267 for Saleh's Iran Party and
National Front setting; `SUP-007`, session 25, 18 May 1950, for the exact
credential act.
