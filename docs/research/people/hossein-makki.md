# Hossein Makki — adviser claim record

Documented role: National Front deputy for Tehran and prominent parliamentary
oil advocate. The adviser abstracts floor and oil-campaign work. It does not
project Makki's later break with Mossadegh backward into 1949–1951. Sources:
`SUP-007`, session 30, 1 June 1950; `MAJ-S2`, chapter 5, pp. 250–267.
