# Ayatollah Abol-Qasem Kashani — counterpart claim record

Documented role: politically active cleric and important mobilizer against
British influence who supported nationalization and Mossadegh before later
becoming an opponent. He did not speak for all clergy. The pinned interface
models negotiation with an independent counterpart, not staff subordination.
Availability at National Front formation is a conservative game gate for
political cooperation, not a claim that Kashani joined a subordinate adviser
roster or was physically present in a common headquarters.
Sources: `MAJ-S2`, chapters 5–6, especially chapter 5, pp. 250–267;
`MAJ-S14`, chapters 1 and 5.
