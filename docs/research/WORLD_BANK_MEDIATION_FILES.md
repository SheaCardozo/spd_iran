# World Bank Iran oil-mediation files

## Outcome

The project no longer treats the World Bank item as “inventory only.” The
official inventory identifies 24 folders in the Records of the Middle East and
North Africa Regional Vice Presidency fonds (`WB IBRD/IDA MNA`). Twenty-three
rows contain legacy `pubdocs.worldbank.org` scan links, but the old links
tested during this search now return World Bank 404 responses after the
document-platform migration. Folder `1806451` never had an embedded scan link.

Two contemporaneous records are now local as
[`SUP-026`](AVAILABLE_SOURCES.md#sup-026):

- the officially migrated 67-page copy of folder `1806440`, World Bank report
  `75976`; and
- Press Release No. 285 with the attached nine-page official review of the
  Bank's negotiations through March 1952, World Bank report `147458`.

The remaining core correspondence, meeting, proposal, calculation, and mission
folders require restored links or copies from the World Bank Archives. They
are identified and formerly digitized, not bibliographically unknown.

## Known restrictions

Two related records are known restricted items rather than unfound files:

- A. S. G. Hoar's 23 November 1951 memorandum, including “Legal Competence of
  Bank to Take Action,” was withheld as attorney-client privileged; appeal
  `AI3487` was dismissed as untimely.
- The Executive Directors' 23 November 1951 transcript was withheld on
  attorney-client and deliberative-information grounds; decision `AI2258`
  explains that the Access to Information Committee could not override the
  Board's disclosure decision.

The decisions are archived in `SUP-026`. They document access status only and
do not establish the substance of the withheld records.

## Accessible corroborating records

`SUP-026` also preserves:

- the 1951–52 annual report, whose Iran discussion is at printed pp. 17–18 /
  PDF pp. 18–19;
- Robert L. Garner's 1961 oral history; and
- Hector Prud'homme's 1985 oral history.

The oral histories are first-person institutional recollections by participants
decades after the negotiations. Their retrospective, self-justifying, and
occasionally prejudicial language requires comparison with contemporaneous
files and scholarship.

The U.S. Department of State's official *Foreign Relations of the United
States* edition provides a separate documentary sequence in volume X,
documents 117–169. Particularly useful cross-checks are documents 134 (the
initial plan), 144 (Prud'homme and Rieber's technical findings), 167 (March
negotiations), and 169 (U.S. response to the Bank's published review).

Nicolas Gorjestani, “The Anglo-Iranian Oil Crisis Re-revisited: World Bank and
Truman–Churchill Proposals,” *British Journal of Middle Eastern Studies*
(published online 2024), supplies document-level citations to folders
`1806432`, `1806447`, `1806448`, `1806453`, and `1806457`. Treat it as a
peer-reviewed scholarly argument and precise archival locator, not a neutral
substitute for the folders.

No historical game claim is approved merely because a folder is listed here.
Implementation still requires review of the underlying item, a precise
document/page locator, and comparison with Iranian and other interested
records.
