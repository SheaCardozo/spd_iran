# Sixteenth Majles and First Senate opening

## Documented anchor

The Sixteenth Majles and First Senate opened in February 1950. The official
proceedings record 98 credentials approved in Majles session 2, followed by
later waves.

## Limits and counterfactual

The game does not present the opening as a settled party-seat chart. Unmapped
member-level credential dates remain unresolved.

## Mechanical abstraction

The opening loads sourced aggregate scenario states into separate place
records; later reducers derive totals from those records.
