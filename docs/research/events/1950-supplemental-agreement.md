# Gass–Golshayan campaign and rejection

## Documented anchor

The supplemental agreement offered additional revenue while leaving the AIOC
structure and control substantially intact. The oil committee rejected it on
25 November 1950 and the Majles on 11 January 1951.

## Limits and counterfactual

The Saudi-Aramco comparison is contextual pressure, not a claim that the
contracts were identical. Unknown detailed terms remain null. Player choices
shape explanation and coalition work, not the recorded rejections.

## Mechanical abstraction

The proposal is a structured term record; no single compromise scalar exists.
