# 1951 confrontation with Razmara

## Documented anchor

Prime Minister Haj Ali Razmara argued that immediate nationalization carried
serious administrative and economic dangers.

## Limits and counterfactual

The scene does not reconstruct unverified dialogue or reduce his position to a
single motive. The player chooses the Front's parliamentary or public answer.

## Mechanical abstraction

Procedure, intelligence, mandate, and oil-coalition effects represent campaign
strategy rather than a measured response to one speech.
