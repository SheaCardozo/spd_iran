# 4 February 1949 attempt and emergency measures

## Documented anchor

An assassin fired at Mohammad Reza Shah on 4 February 1949. The government
then imposed martial law, suppressed the Tudeh Party, arrested opponents, and
convened a constituent assembly.

## Limits and counterfactual

The game does not assign responsibility beyond the assailant or convert later
repression into proof of a wider plot. Player choices concern evidentiary
caution, legal documentation, and preserving contacts; they cannot prevent the
documented emergency measures.

## Mechanical abstraction

The January recurring cards and the February–April legal, correspondence, and
consultation cards change legitimacy, organization, press capacity,
parliamentary procedure, or Crown relations. These are scenario values, not
historical measurements. The attempt and immediate emergency sequence resolve
as the first dated event after January's ordinary action.
