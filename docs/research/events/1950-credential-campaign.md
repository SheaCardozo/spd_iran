# 1950 National Front credential campaign

## Documented anchor

Credential approvals came in successive chamber decisions. Saleh's
Kashan/Natanz credential passed on 18 May; the eleven Tehran credentials
passed on 1 June.

## Limits and counterfactual

The scene does not infer a deputy's ideology from credential timing or treat
the eight National Front returns as eight simultaneous February votes.

## Mechanical abstraction

Player strategy affects procedural legitimacy, relationships, and influence,
not historical credential evidence.
