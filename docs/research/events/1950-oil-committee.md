# 1950 Majles oil committee

## Documented anchor

An eighteen-member oil committee including five National Front deputies gave
the small delegation a platform for scrutiny of the supplemental agreement.

## Limits and counterfactual

The player's coalition-building emphasis cannot change committee creation or
invent a permanent party majority.

## Mechanical abstraction

Oil-coalition support and parliamentary-procedure legitimacy summarize
scenario capacity; they do not replace committee membership or recorded votes.
