# November 1949 National Front structure

## Documented anchor

Cooperation around the palace protest developed into the National Front, a
coalition rather than a disciplined mass party.

## Limits and counterfactual

The choices among a narrow program, a loose coordination committee, and a
membership-based coalition are counterfactual emphases within documented
organizational limits. They do not claim that any exact charter historically
existed.

## Mechanical abstraction

The choice changes component relations and dissent, while aggregate cohesion
is derived centrally.
