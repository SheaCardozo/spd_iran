# March 1951 nationalization approvals

## Documented anchor

The oil committee approved nationalization after Razmara's assassination, the
Majles approved the principle in mid-March, and the Senate approved it on
20 March.

## Limits and counterfactual

Passage is fixed in v0.1. The scene does not invent a numeric Majles division
or treat the principle as if it already settled the later implementation law,
personnel, marketing, compensation, or interim finance.

## Mechanical abstraction

The player changes the institutional and organizational condition of the
coalition at passage. Structured unknown terms remain null.

## Sources

- `SUP-007`, Sixteenth Majles session 128, 15 March 1951.
- `SUP-059`, official Special Oil Commission minutes, print-order no. 300,
  second-period record, for the committee's route to the nationalization
  recommendation.

`SUP-006`, printed pp. 15–16, records the later nine-article implementation
law. It is a boundary source for what this scene does **not** claim and is not
cited as evidence for approval of the March principle. The controlling First
Senate session 84 scan is not locally accessible; the game therefore states

synthesis and enacted sequence. It does not invent attendance, a division,
speeches, or named commitments. See
