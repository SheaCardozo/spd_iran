# 7 March 1951 assassination of Razmara

## Documented anchor

Khalil Tahmasabi, a member of Fada'iyan-e Islam, assassinated Razmara on
7 March 1951.

## Limits and counterfactual

Who else encouraged or knew about the killing remains unresolved. The game
does not assign collective responsibility. The player controls only the
Front's bounded public response.

## Mechanical abstraction

Legitimacy and coalition effects model the response's political cost; the
assassination and its date cannot vary.
