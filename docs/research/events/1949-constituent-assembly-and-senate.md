# 1949 constituent assembly and Senate preparation

## Documented anchor

The constituent assembly strengthened the Shah's constitutional position,
including a dissolution power under revised Article 48, while the new Senate
combined elected and appointed routes.

## Limits and counterfactual

The prologue lets the player frame an objection or prepare for institutional
risk. It does not reverse the constitutional revision or invent a homogeneous
opposition.

## Mechanical abstraction

`shah_resistance`, `constitutional_legitimacy`, and election preparation are
forward-looking scenario state. Senate place records preserve route separately
from mutable usability and support.
