# November 1949 assassination of Abdolhossein Hazhir

## Documented anchor

Hazhir's assassination occurred during the election crisis from which the
National Front emerged. Azimi's account places Hazhir on the Court side of the
election conflict; the scene's royalist semantic treatment represents that
institutional alignment, not a permanent party label.

## Limits and counterfactual

The scene deliberately makes no unsupported claim about National Front
responsibility, prior knowledge, or a single causal effect on the Tehran
rerun. The player controls only the Front's public response.

## Mechanical abstraction

Legitimacy, parliamentary-procedure, press, and faction-relation effects
express the cost of a response strategy rather than measured contemporary
opinion.
