# Participant memoir and documentary-source acquisition audit

- **Search and intake dates:** 2026-07-28; local-file updates 2026-07-29
- **Scope:** the eight highest-priority participant memoir, correspondence, and
  documentary-edition additions identified for individual, factional, Iranian,
  and coup-side decision-making
- **Outcome:** five initial local archive objects acquired under
  `SUP-071`–`SUP-075`, followed on 2026-07-29 by Ebtehaj's combined
  two-volume memoir as `SUP-078`, a Makki background volume as `SUP-079`, and
  an incomplete Makki coup account as `SUP-080`. A second-best pass then added
  Bazargan's official collected works and Nejati-mediated memoir as
  `SUP-081`–`SUP-082`; the remaining works are divided between genuine
  acquisition gaps and access-controlled or search-only readers

## Method and access rule

Searches used Persian title and author variants, Iran Archive, Ketabnak,
library catalogs, publisher and bookseller records, Open Library, and the
internal cross-references of located texts. A catalog page, online-reader
landing page, or CAPTCHA response was not saved as if it were the book.

The local archive preserves unchanged carrier bytes. Verification means
format parsing, bibliographic matching against title or publication matter,
page or sequence inspection, checksum recording, and explicit limits. It does
not mean every historical assertion has been accepted or every page has been
read.

### Integration status

Archive intake and initial structural inspection do not make all of these
works claim-ready. `SUP-071`–`SUP-075`, `SUP-078`–`SUP-080`, and `SUP-082`
remain inputs for page-level extraction and cross-position comparison. Their
descriptions above identify potential research roles, not findings already
incorporated into the timeline, crisis clock, cabinet model, parliamentary
ledger, or economic synthesis.

The exception is the bounded review of `SUP-081` recorded above and in the
[Ketabnak substitute-evidence map](KETABNAK_SECOND_BEST_ALTERNATIVES_2026-07-29.md):
the specified oil-dispossession ranges were checked to map coverage and
transmission. Claims from other sections still require their own locators.
OCR derivatives remain discovery aids; completing OCR does not promote a
memoir claim or make related transmissions independent corroboration.

control was not attempted.

## Genuine remaining acquisition gaps

### Mohammad Nasser Qashqai, *Salhā-ye bohrān*

- **Full title:** *Salhā-ye bohrān: khāterāt-e ruzāneh-ye Mohammad Naser
  Sowlat Qashqāʾi, az Farvardin 1329 tā Āzar 1332*.
- **Edition:** Edited by Nasrollah Haddadi; Rasa, first edition 1366 SH /
  1987–88, 551 pp.
- **Identifier:** no valid ISBN located; CiNii **NCID `BA67968060`** for the
  first edition and [`BB10783459`](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB10783459) for
  the third. [First-edition catalog record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA67968060).
- **Result:** no complete online text found. `SUP-061` contains Qashqai's
  seven-tape official Harvard oral history. A complete third-party Persian web
  transcript is now preserved within that local collection as a search aid,
  but it names no transcriber or method and has eight headings for seven
  Harvard audio objects; exact language must be verified against official
  audio and a recorded timecode. With `MAJ-S19`, parliamentary records,
  diplomatic reporting, and coup scholarship, the [bounded substitute
  stack](SECOND_BEST_EVIDENCE_STACKS_2026-07-29.md#3-mohammad-nasser-qashqai-salhā-ye-bohrān)
  supports broad relationships and choices. It does not replace the diary's
  dated chronology or wording.

### Shams al-Din Amir-Alai, oil rebuttal

- **Correct title:** *Naqdī bar ketāb Khalʿ-e yad: Ketāb-e sīyāh, yā,
  Khaṭṭ-e sīyāhī bar Ketāb-e sīyāh*
  (نقدی بر کتاب خلع ید: کتاب سیاه، یا، خط سیاهی بر کتاب سیاه).
- **Record:** first published around 1981; [Open Library author
  record](https://openlibrary.org/authors/OL1066187A/Shams_al-Di%CC%84n_Amir_%CA%BBAla%CC%84%CA%BCi%CC%84).
- **Identifier:** no ISBN located.
- **Result:** no complete online text found.
- **Correction:** *Khalʿ-e yad az Sherkat-e Naft-e Engelis va Iran* is
  Hossein Makki's *Ketab-e sīyāh* material. It must not be attributed to
  Amir-Alai.

### Kashani collection, volumes I–II

- **Title:** *Majmuʿeh-i az maktubat, sokhanraniha va payamha-ye Ayatollah
  Kashani*, compiled by Mohammad Dehnavi.
- **Result:** no online copies of volumes I or II were found. Volume II has
  **NCID `BA67624673`** and **LCCN `84196205`** in the [CiNii catalog
  record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA67624673). No dependable
  volume-specific ISBN was located.
- **Priority:** volume II first, then volume I; volume III is already
  identifiable through the access-controlled reader above.
- **Series correction:** the Dehnavi collection contains five volumes.
  Volumes III and IV are also represented by digitized-but-restricted
  HathiTrust records, and Ketabnak book `100209` identifies volume V as the
  351-page *Istidrākāt* or addenda volume. Volume V is lower priority than the
  campaign-period volumes.

## Acquisition priority after the second-best pass

1. Qashqai's dated diary.
2. Makki's *Vaqāyeʿ-e Sī Tir* and political memoir.
3. Amir-Alai's rebuttal to Makki, because an opposing oil-participant position
   adds more than another general operational narrative.
4. Kashani volume II and then I for the pre-coup public and organizational
   sequence.
5. Individual missing newspaper issues or exact documents required by a
   planned claim.
6. Kashani volume III and Makki's oil volumes for exact wording and
   participant framing; useful but no longer general blockers.
7. Printed pp. 425–440 of Makki's coup book for exact document provenance.
8. Kashani volumes IV–V only for a post-coup extension or an addenda
   cross-reference.
9. Print facsimiles of the Sadighi texts in Nejati or Varjavand for
   transcription verification.

These participant works supplement rather than replace the book-length
scholarly foundation. The detailed substitution and non-equivalence rules are
