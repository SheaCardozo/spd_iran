# Reader-first historical synthesis

- **Date:** 2026-07-27
- **Status:** Implemented

## Summary

The later half of the standalone historical primer was revised to explain
uncertainty and causation directly rather than as a survey of scholarly
positions. The revision removes named historian-versus-historian comparisons,
defensive phrases about what the evidence “does not establish,” and headings
that frame the narrative as a historiographical debate.

The new prose states what happened, what remains unknown, and why it mattered.
“Coup or popular uprising?” is now “How the coup succeeded,” and
“Interpretive spine” is now the reader-facing conclusion “Why the crisis ended
in a coup.” The Afshartus section also states the absence of a documented
foreign role directly rather than referring prematurely to TPAJAX, which is
introduced only in the following section.

## Reason

The primer is for politically literate readers who may know little about Iran
or its scholarly literature. Phrases such as “the principal scholarly dispute”
made the page sound as though it were preemptively answering an academic
controversy rather than orienting a new reader. Historical uncertainty still
needs to be visible, but it is clearer when expressed as a concrete limit:
the exact toll is unknown, a chain of command cannot be reconstructed, or a
specific connection was not documented.

## Dynamic SPD comparison

- **Reference paths:** `source/scenes/events/sa_ban_pre.scene.dry`;
  `source/scenes/events/presidential_election_1932_candidate.scene.dry`; and
  `source/scenes/events/hitler_naturalization.scene.dry`.
- **What Dynamic SPD does:** event prose presents actions, motives, allegations,
  and uncertainty in the narrative voice. It rarely pauses to identify
  historians or summarize competing schools of interpretation for the player.
- **Decision here:** retain that direct narrative convention while preserving
  The Last Majles's stricter claim-level citations and explicit limits on
  certainty. The bibliography remains the place for author expertise and
  interpretive differences.
- **Divergence level:** local. This is an editorial presentation change, not a
  change to game or build architecture.

## System fit

`docs/research/TIMELINE_PRIMER.md` remains the canonical text. The timeline
builder now recognizes the renamed conclusion and labels it “Conclusion.”
The integrated and standalone browser outputs are regenerated by the normal
build. A UI regression rejects the most conspicuous literature-review phrases
and the superseded headings.

## Research and assets

No source or claim was removed. Existing citations remain adjacent to the
reframed claims, including the different operational reconstructions of
19 August. No assets or rights records changed.
